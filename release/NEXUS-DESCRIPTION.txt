[size=5][b]Easy Steer[/b][/size]
[b]One-Stick Movement and Auto-Follow Camera[/b]

Steer your character. Let the camera follow.

Easy Steer lets you explore Skyrim with WASD or one movement stick. Left and right turn your character, forward and back move along that heading, and the third-person camera follows smoothly. First person uses the same steering with its normal direct view.

[b]How it plays[/b]
[list]
[*]A/D or left-stick X turns your character, including while standing still.
[*]W/S or left-stick Y moves forward and backward.
[*]Hold forward and turn together to move along a curved path.
[*]Release movement to keep your new facing. The third-person camera finishes settling behind you.
[*]Both perspectives are enabled by default. Switch either back to normal controls independently.
[/list]

These are classic tank-style controls: left/right replaces strafing while steering is enabled. Keyboard movement remappings are respected. Mouse and right-stick look remain available, with configurable override behaviour in third person.

[b]Weapons and aiming[/b]
Steering works with weapons drawn. Active bow/crossbow aiming and directed spell casting temporarily restore normal movement and camera controls. Existing combat mechanics and animations are used; Easy Steer does not add target lock or auto-aim. Precision aiming and vertical look may still require the mouse or right stick.

[b]Requirements[/b]
[list]
[*]Windows x64 Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition.
[*][url=https://skse.silverlock.org/]SKSE64[/url] matching your game executable.
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/32444]Address Library for SKSE Plugins[/url] matching your runtime.
[/list]
Gameplay tested by the author on Steam Skyrim 1.7.104.0. Other SE/AE runtimes are not independently validated. Skyrim VR and original Skyrim are not supported. No ESP/ESL, Papyrus, SkyUI, MCM or animation framework is needed.

[b]Installation[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Close Skyrim and install the requirements above.
[*]Install the Easy Steer main archive through Vortex or Mod Organizer 2.
[*]Enable it, deploy in Vortex, and launch Skyrim through SKSE.
[/list]
For manual installation, copy the archive's SKSE folder into Skyrim's Data folder. No ESP needs enabling.

[b]Upgrading from Auto-Follow Camera[/b]
Disable/uninstall the old mod and deploy its removal before enabling Easy Steer. AutoFollowCamera.dll must no longer be present in Data/SKSE/Plugins. Easy Steer refuses to enable while the old DLL remains, preventing duplicate steering. Copy custom settings from the old INI into EasySteer.ini.

[b]Configuration[/b]
In Vortex, open the mod's staging folder and edit SKSE/Plugins/EasySteer.ini. Deploy and restart Skyrim after editing.

[code][EasySteer]
TankControls=true
FirstPersonSteering=true
ThirdPersonSteering=true
TurnSpeed=120.0[/code]

Set FirstPersonSteering=false for normal first-person controls. Set ThirdPersonSteering=false for normal third-person controls and camera behaviour. These switches are independent; keep TankControls=true when using them.

Other settings control turn speed, controller deadzone, third-person follow speed, optional pitch, manual-look override and an optional recenter key. First person has no trailing camera lag. The included README explains every setting.

[b]Compatibility and limitations[/b]
Easy Steer changes movement input and camera rotation. Compatibility with True Directional Movement, SmoothCam and other movement/camera overhauls is not established; competing controllers may conflict. Steering is suspended in menus, dialogue, mounts, scripted camera states and incapacitation. It simplifies navigation rather than replacing every input needed to play Skyrim.

[b]Troubleshooting[/b]
Check EasySteer.log under Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE. For a bug report, enable DebugLogging in the INI, restart, reproduce the issue and include the log, runtime version, input device and relevant mods. Turn diagnostics off afterward.
